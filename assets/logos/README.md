# Logo sources

- Python, Java, Spring, TypeScript, JavaScript, React, Node.js, Blender, Unreal Engine, After Effects and Premiere Pro: [Skill Icons](https://github.com/tandpfun/skill-icons), used under the included MIT license. SVG files are unmodified.
- ComfyUI: Comfy's yellow brand icon, from the official brand assets.
- FFmpeg: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/ffmpeg.svg), distributed under [CC0](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/LICENSE.md), served in green by the Simple Icons CDN.
- ElevenLabs: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons/blob/develop/icons/elevenlabs.svg), under CC0, served in white and black by the Simple Icons CDN for dark and light themes.
- Higgsfield: the unmodified [official site icon](https://higgsfield.ai/icon.png).
- Claude, Ollama and NVIDIA: [Simple Icons](https://github.com/simple-icons/simple-icons), under CC0; Claude and NVIDIA use brand colors, and Ollama has black/white theme variants.
- OpenAI: the unmodified avatar of the [official OpenAI GitHub organization](https://github.com/openai), linked from [OpenAI's website](https://openai.com/brand/).

Product names and logos belong to their respective owners. Their inclusion identifies tools used in these projects and does not imply endorsement.
